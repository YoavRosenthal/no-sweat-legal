Privacy Policy

No Sweat · Last updated 29 August 2026

The short version

No Sweat is a marketplace for reselling boutique fitness class reservations. We collect what it takes to run that marketplace and nothing else. We do not sell your data, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Account. Your email address, and a password we never see in plain text.
  • Profile. Your name, date of birth, gender and mobile number. The date of birth is there because you must be 18 to use No Sweat; the rest is how a studio knows who walked in.
  • Listings. When you list a class, you upload a screenshot of your booking confirmation. We read the studio, class, time and the name on the reservation out of it, and we keep the image only as long as the listing needs it.
  • Payments. Card details go straight to Stripe and never touch our servers. We keep the amount, the date and Stripe's identifier for the payment. If you cash out, Stripe also collects the identity information US financial regulations require of anyone receiving money.
  • Waivers. When you sign the participant agreement we store what you typed, when, from which IP address, and which version of the app and the document.
  • Device token. If you turn on alerts, an anonymous push token so we can tell you a class you wanted is listed.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your photo library beyond the screenshot you choose, or any advertising identifier.

What other people see

Before a spot is paid for, a buyer sees your first name and last initial — nothing more. The full name on the reservation is revealed only after payment has settled, and only to the person who bought it, because that is the name they will give at the door. Your email, your phone number and your date of birth are never shown to another user.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe — payments in and payouts out.
  • Supabase — our database and file storage.
  • Anthropic — the booking screenshot is sent to Claude to read the class details out of it. It is not used to train any model.
  • Apple — push notifications, and Apple Pay if you pay that way.

That is the whole list. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Deleting your account

Open the Me tab and tap Delete my account. It deletes, it does not deactivate. Your profile, your saved cash-out destinations, your alerts and your uploaded screenshots are erased.

Classes you have already bought or sold stay on the books, with your name removed from them. We keep those records because a trade is two people — the other side of it is somebody else's receipt — and because financial records have to be retained. They cannot be traced back to you.

We will refuse to delete an account while money is unresolved: a spot still listed, a class still in escrow, a cash-out on its way, or a balance we would then have no way to send you. The app tells you which, and each has a way out.

Children

No Sweat is for people 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect anything from anyone younger, and the database refuses a date of birth under 18.

Your rights

You can see and correct your profile in the app at any time, and delete your account as described above. If you are in California, the EU or the UK and want a copy of what we hold, or want something corrected that the app will not let you change, email us and we will do it.
