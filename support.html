I was turned away at the door

Open the Me tab, find the class, and tap Report a problem. You have until an hour after the class starts. The refund is automatic and comes back the way you paid — nobody has to approve it.

Where is my money?

A sale credits your No Sweat balance an hour after the class starts, once the report window has closed. From the balance you can cash out to a bank, which usually takes one to two business days, or spend it on another class.

Why do you need my Social Security number?

Only to pay you. Stripe collects it the first time you cash out, because US law requires anyone sending money to verify who is receiving it. We never see it — it goes straight to Stripe. Buying a class never asks for it.

My listing was blocked

A booking screenshot can only be listed once. If yours was refused as a duplicate, the same confirmation is already on the market — check whether you listed it before.

Deleting your account

Me → Delete my account. Cash out your balance first; we cannot send it to you afterwards. See the privacy policy for exactly what is erased and what is kept.
